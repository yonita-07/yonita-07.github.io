Urban Mobility Delay Analysis

End-to-end machine learning project predicting train delays across 20 German cities using 77K records

Project Overview

A complete end-to-end data science project analyzing train delays across Germany's 20 largest city stations. Built a binary classification model achieving 84.89% accuracy to predict delay likelihood.

77K Train Records

Comprehensive dataset covering July-September 2024 across 20 major German stations

84.89% Accuracy

Random Forest model with hyperparameter tuning achieves strong predictive performance

Actionable Insights

Clear hourly and station-based delay patterns identified for operational improvements

Dataset Overview

Time Period

July – September 2024

Coverage

20 Largest City Main Stations (Hbf) in Germany

Total Records

~77,000 train observations

Delay Rate

~22% of trains experienced delays

Key Features:

date

Timestamp of scheduled departure

Hbf (station)

Departure station identifier

train_model

Type of train service

route

Train route information

scheduled_time / expected_time

Timing information

has_delay (target)

1 = delayed, 0 = on time

Key Insights from Analysis

Delay Distribution

Approximately 22% of all trains experienced delays during the observation period.

This baseline provides context for model performance and operational planning.

Rush Hour Patterns

Delay rates peak during morning and evening rush hours, correlating with increased operational load.

Time-of-day is a strong predictor of delay likelihood.

Station Variations

Some stations consistently show higher delay frequencies, indicating infrastructure or operational bottlenecks.

Station-level analysis reveals priority areas for intervention.

Daily Trends

Delay rates fluctuate based on weekday patterns and operational load variations.

Day-of-week features could improve model performance.

Operational Insight

Station location, route complexity, and train model all significantly influence delay probability. These factors can be used to prioritize infrastructure investments and operational interventions for maximum impact on system reliability.
